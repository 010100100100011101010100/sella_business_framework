##  Curriculum Scope (6 Exhaustive Chapters)

1. **Chapter 1: Vectors, Calculus & Advanced 2D Kinematics**
   - Relative motion, optimal river-swimmer drift calculus, rain-man vector trigonometry.
   - Projectiles on inclined planes (uphill/downhill range extrema, condition for perpendicular collision).
   - Intrinsic coordinates, radius of curvature $\rho = v^2/a_\perp$, tangential and normal acceleration components.
   - Polar coordinates ($v_r, v_\theta, a_r, a_\theta$) and non-constant jerk analysis.

2. **Chapter 2: Newton's Laws & Complex Friction Dynamics**
   - Non-inertial reference frames, pseudo-force vector transformations, accelerating elevators.
   - Multi-block static friction thresholding hierarchies and sequential slipping transitions.
   - Pulley-wedge kinematic constraint differential calculus.
   - Normal force redistribution on accelerating wedges and toppling thresholds.

3. **Chapter 3: Work, Energy & Power Mastery**
   - Generalized Work-Energy Theorem in inertial and non-inertial frames with pseudo-work.
   - Conservative vector field curl criteria ($\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}$) and line integral path independence.
   - Potential energy landscapes $U(x)$, non-linear equilibrium stability, higher-order derivatives, and small oscillation frequencies $\omega = \sqrt{U''(x_0)/m}$.
   - Vertical Circular Motion (VCM): light string vs rigid rod, tension slackening ($T = 0$) vs velocity vanishing ($v = 0$), projectile departure in the upper semicircle, and the universal tension delta $T_{bottom} - T_{top} = 6mg$.
   - Power-limited engine dynamics, launch traction thresholds ($a_{\max} = \mu g$), and the universal 50% thermal dissipation paradox on conveyor belts.

4. **Chapter 4: Center of Mass, Linear Momentum & Collisions**
   - Continuous mass distributions: first-principles calculus derivations for semicircular wire ($2R/\pi$), semicircular disc ($4R/3\pi$), hemispherical shell ($R/2$), solid hemisphere ($3R/8$), hollow cone ($h/3$), and solid cone ($h/4$).
   - Center of Mass frame (zero-momentum C-frame) and Koenig's Kinetic Energy Decomposition Theorem ($K = K_{cm} + K_{rel}$).
   - Reduced mass formulation ($\mu = \frac{m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2}$) for two-body systems.
   - Oblique collisions: Line of Impact (LOI) vs Common Tangential Plane (COT), restitution along LOI ($e = \frac{v_{2n}-v_{1n}}{u_{1n}-u_{2n}}$), tangential momentum conservation, and energy dissipation.
   - Variable mass mechanics: Meshchersky Equation, Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation with gravity and drag, leaking freight cars, and the 3x weight factor of falling chains ($N = 3\lambda g x$).

5. **Chapter 5: Rotational Mechanics & Toppling Dynamics**
   - Moment of Inertia tensor, Steiner's Parallel Axis Theorem ($I = I_{cm} + Md_\perp^2$), and the Perpendicular Axis Theorem ($I_z = I_x + I_y$) with rigorous proof of 3D non-planar failure.
   - Generalized torque relation about accelerating points: $\boldsymbol{\tau}_O = I_O \boldsymbol{\alpha}$ conditions and pseudo-torque.
   - Pure rolling kinematics: contact point velocity is zero, but centripetal acceleration is $\omega^2 R$ upward!
   - Pure rolling dynamics on inclined planes: acceleration $a_{cm} = \frac{g\sin\theta}{1 + k^2/R^2}$, static friction direction, and critical slipping angle.
   - Toppling vs. Sliding mechanics: normal reaction shift $x_N \le b/2$, critical toppling force $F = \frac{b}{2y}Mg$, and competitive failure modes.
   - Center of Percussion: sweet spot of suspension ($d = \frac{I_{hinge}}{M d_{cm}}$) for zero impulsive hinge reaction.

6. **Chapter 6: Gravitation & Oscillatory Motion (SHM)**
   - Gravitational fields and potentials for spherical shells and solid spheres: proof that potential at Earth's center is $-1.5 GM/R$.
   - Gravitational self-energy integrals: thin shell ($-GM^2/2R$) vs solid sphere ($-0.6 GM^2/R$) and interstellar cloud collapse.
   - Orbital mechanics: Kepler's laws, Vis-Viva energy relation $v^2 = GM(2/r - 1/a)$, and the satellite reverse-thrust acceleration paradox.
   - Advanced SHM: two-block spring oscillators ($\omega = \sqrt{k/\mu}$), heavy spring mass correction ($m_{eff} = M + \frac{1}{3}m_s$).
   - Compound physical pendulum: time period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{k^2+d^2}{gd}}$, minimum period at $d = k_{cm}$, and conjugate centers of oscillation (Kater's pendulum).

### 2. Exporting to a 50+ Page PDF Book
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Brave.
2. Press <kbd>Cmd + P</kbd> (macOS) or <kbd>Ctrl + P</kbd> (Windows/Linux).
3. Select **Save as PDF** as destination.
4. Settings:
   - **Paper Size:** A4
   - **Margins:** Default or Minimum
   - **Options:** Check **Background graphics** (to preserve the soft warm paper theme and colored callouts).
5. Click **Save**. The `@media print` CSS engine automatically applies page breaks, suppresses the sidebar, formats running headers, and outputs an elegant 50+ page physical handbook!
