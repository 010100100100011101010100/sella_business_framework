# Autonomous Business Framework

Choose and run a **boring, proven, cash-generating small business** for a human operator.
Optimized for sustainable revenue, not exponential growth.

## Read in this order — do not read everything

1. **`00-core/reasoning-guardrails.md`** — the rules. First, always
2. **`00-core/initiation.md`** — interview or ideation, plus the self-selection algorithm
3. **`00-core/case-registry.md`** — index to the ten businesses, lean tiering, distribution ranking
4. **The one or two case studies** selection points you to. **Not all ten**
5. **`00-core/viability-screen.md`** — pass/fail before committing
6. **`00-core/human-action-queue.md`** — the boundary, and how to use your operator well

Then as the work requires: `00-core/delivery-model-selector.md` ·
`00-core/operating-cadence.md` · `10-resources/business-resources.md` (routes to the
twelve resource files) · `20-systems/` · `30-dashboards/` · `50-guardrails/`

**Reading everything is a failure mode.** `business-resources.md` routes you only to what
this business actually needs; record what you read in `.claude/required-reads.md`.

## The two things that decide outcomes

**Distribution is the business.** You will not fail at production. You will fail at
finding people who will pay. Rank every option by how hard the buyer is to reach, before
ranking by anything else — it carries ×3 weight in the selection algorithm.

**Take maximum assistance from your human.** They own identity, reach, relationships,
credibility, and existing accounts you cannot manufacture. Most agents ask far too little.
Run the full asset inventory in `initiation.md` §2 before planning anything.

## Hard rules

- Never fabricate testimonials, metrics, credentials, or outreach triggers (G3)
- Sell before you build (G2)
- Write kill criteria before starting; read them, do not recall them (G7)
- You are not the legal person — maintain the human action queue (G9)
- Report losses as loudly as wins (G12)
- Never accept a customer's credentials

## Outputs, before any other work

`BUSINESS-CHARTER.md` · `HUMAN-ACTION-QUEUE.md` · `DECISION-LOG.md` · the `.claude/`,
`.cursor/`, and `.antigravity/` manifests. Templates in `templates/`.
